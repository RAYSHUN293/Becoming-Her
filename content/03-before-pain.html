Her Warmth
The way Albertha loves people is rare. She makes others feel seen without even trying. That warmth was never something she learned — it was always just her.
The things that make her eyes light up
Her Wonder
She has always found beauty in places most people walk past. The music that stops her mid-sentence. The small things that make an ordinary day feel like something more.
Quiet but powerful
Her Softness
There is a gentleness in Albertha that the world does not always appreciate. But it is one of the most powerful things about her. Softness like hers takes real courage to keep.
She has always been this
Albertha, Simply Herself
Before anyone had opinions about who she should be, Albertha already was someone remarkable. Expressive. Passionate. Deeply feeling. Someone who cares so completely it shows in everything she does. That is not something she became. That is who she has always been.
"She was never too much. The world just wasn't ready for all of her."
