IVFuture Her
Chapter Four

The Albertha
she is becoming

Close your eyes and picture her. She is grounded. She knows who she is outside of anyone else's opinion. She still loves deeply — but from a place of fullness, not fear. She knows her value and she does not need to earn it. She is you, Albie. Already on her way. Already arriving.

Identity
She Knows Herself
She has passions that are entirely her own. She knows what she likes, what she stands for, what she wants from life and she does not need anyone's validation to claim it.
"Knowing yourself is the quietest, most powerful thing."
Peace
She Is at Peace
She has learned that her emotions are valid AND that they do not have to overwhelm her. She feels everything fully and she is okay. She is steady inside herself, even when things around her are not.
"Feeling deeply is a gift. Peace is learning to hold it gently."
Security
She Trusts Herself
She no longer needs constant reassurance because she has built a quiet inner knowing. She knows she is loved. She knows she is worthy. And she lets that be a joy not a need she is afraid to lose.
"Security doesn't come from being held tight. It grows quietly within."
Wholeness
She Loves Freely
She loves her boyfriend deeply and genuinely and she loves herself too. She has her own world, her own light. When they come together, it is not need meeting need. It is two whole people who choose each other.
"The most beautiful love is between two people who are also complete on their own."
"She is not who she was. She is not yet who she will be.
But right now, in this exact becoming — Albertha is the most alive she has ever been."
